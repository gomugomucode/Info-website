WHAT ISNUCLEI ?
Nuclei is a powerfulopen - sourcevulnerability scanner written in Golang . Aside from its
excellentperformance , it is a highly customizable tool due to its integration with YAML
templatefiles . Its templating system allows for anyone to create vulnerability templates
and contribute their findings to thecommunity !
